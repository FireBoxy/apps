    Quick guide

    Everything happens in one pane — no split view. Formatting shows up right where you type.

    • #
      Format headings. Start a line with # (H1), ## or -# (H2), or ### / -## (H3) to format progressively smaller headings.
    • ✍️
      Toolbar & shortcuts. Use the toolbar or Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I for bold and italic.
    • 🔗
      Links with Ctrl/⌘ + K. Select any word, press Ctrl/⌘ + K, and paste a URL — it becomes a blue underlined link.
    • 📺
      Embeds inline. Paste a link on its own line and it becomes an embed right in the editor. Hover it and press ✕ to close it.
    • 🗂
      Folders & tags. Organize in the sidebar. Tag suggestions are one click away.
    • ⏰
      More tools. Open ⋯ in the editor for reminders, version history, export, share links and encrypted backups.
    • ⌨️
      Ctrl+F search · Ctrl+S save · Ctrl+N new note · Esc close
